Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs: Beat eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the pan: Melt butter over medium heat.
  3. Cook: Pour eggs into the pan and cook evenly.
  4. Add fillings: Add fillings before folding.
  5. Fold: Fold the omelette in half and cook briefly.
  6. Serve: Enjoy while warm.
